1 Wallpaper

Factory Butte in Wayne County, Utah was named after is resemblance to a Factory. At 6,302 ft tall, it’s a humbling feeling the closer you get to it. It makes me smile every time I see it. The fact early settlers named it Factory Butte because of its shape and man has never built something this big is both ironic and hilarious.

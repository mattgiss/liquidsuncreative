Something for you. Issue#13

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

Book: Unfuck Yourself by Gary John Bishop
Bishop challenges the idea that transformation starts with belief. He argues it begins with responsibility. You don’t have to wait to feel empowered—you just have to own your reality. The mind can spin stories, but only your actions write the truth. This is about breaking the contract with your excuses and signing one with your future self.

1 Wallpaper

“Canyon Radiance”
Where stone meets sky, the earth stretches wide in a quiet kind of joy. Sunlight dances off ancient cliffs, painting shadows that feel alive. Every curve, every crack hums with possibility.

1 Quote From Others

""I am not my thoughts; I am what I do." —Yourself

1 Question For You

What bold action have you been postponing?

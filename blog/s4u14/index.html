Something for you. Issue#14

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

Book: Be Water, My Friend by Shannon Lee
Bruce Lee’s philosophy wasn’t just about martial arts—it was about mastering the art of living. In Be Water, My Friend, Shannon Lee distills her father’s teachings into something elemental: adaptability isn’t weakness, it’s wisdom in motion.

To be like water is to embrace change, not out of passivity but with intention. Water doesn’t force its way forward—it senses, reshapes, and redirects. It will wear down mountains, slip through cracks, and still reflect light with perfect clarity.

In a world that demands certainty, Bruce Lee invites us into presence. He reminds us that identity is fluid, not fixed. That our strength is not in rigidity, but in response. True power, he teaches, is the ability to move through life—not armored, but aware.

To “be water” is not to retreat. It’s to advance—calmly, creatively, and completely free.

1 Wallpaper

Lily Lake sits quietly beneath the watchful rise of Estes Cone, reflecting the sky like a whispered secret. The water mirrors pine-draped slopes and jagged granite ridges, blurring the line between earth and sky. This is a place where stillness isn't silence—it's clarity. A gentle reminder that power doesn’t always roar. Sometimes, it flows, listens, and reflects.

1 Quote From Others

"Be formless, shapeless, like water... Water can flow or it can crash. Be water, my friend." - Bruce Lee

1 Question For You

Where in your life are you clenching too hard? What would happen if you softened your grip?

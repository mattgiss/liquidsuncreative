Something for you. Issue#20

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

Book: "The Dip" by Seth Godin

Most people get quitting all wrong. They bail too early, hang on too long, or never let go at all. Seth Godin’s The Dip is a tight, no-nonsense lesson in recognizing that rough patch after the honeymoon phase and before real mastery. Winners know when to dig in and when to cut their losses. If you are stuck in something going nowhere, quit fast. If you are in a tough stretch on the road to excellence, push through it. The problem is most people cannot tell the difference, and that is exactly why they stay average. Half the world is busy quietly quitting while the other half is burning out from relentless hustle. Neither approach is smart. The Dip argues you need courage, but more importantly, discernment. It is not about working harder; it is about knowing what is actually worth it.

1 Wallpaper

1 Quote From Others

"Winners quit the wrong stuff. They stick with the right stuff. They have the guts to turn down mediocre opportunities" — Seth Godin

1 Question For You

What’s one thing you’re doing right now that deserves to be abandoned? And what’s one thing so valuable you should double down, no matter how hard it is?

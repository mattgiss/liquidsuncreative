Something for you. Issue#23

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

Creativity is a Habit, Not a Perk. Chase Jarvis says creativity is not a gift for a chosen few. It is a muscle you build through practice, not something you wait on until inspiration shows up. His IDEA framework — Imagine, Design, Execute, Amplify — reminds us that thinking about something is not enough. You have to make it, share it, and keep going. Believing in yourself means showing up for your ideas every day. Even when they feel small. Even when nobody is paying attention. That is how you move from wishing to creating.

1 Quote From Others

“Creators create. Action is identity. You become what you do. You don’t need permission from anybody to call yourself a writer, entrepreneur, or musician. You just need to write, build a business, or make music. You’ve got to do the verb to be the noun.” — Chase Jarvis

1 Question For You

What is one thing you can make today that is just for you?

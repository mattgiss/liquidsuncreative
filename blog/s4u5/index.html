Something for you. Issue#5

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

"This week, I’m taking a break from work to go home and see my family in Georgia. Just like my camping trips, time with family and vacations are a great way to reset. I’m excited to see everyone and be home. Family reunions remind me of the simple joys in life—being present, sharing stories, and creating memories. It’s a chance to slow down and appreciate the people who matter most."

1 Wallpaper

Lily, Lake in Estes Park is one of my favorite weekend getaways. I’ve gone there when I’m happy, sad, nervous, and proud. It’s a place where I can sit zazen and look out and see elevation changes. Kind of like life.

1 Quote From Others

"The best thing to spend on your children is your time." — Arnold Glasow

1 Question For You

"When was the last time you took a moment to reconnect with your loved ones, and how did it make you feel?"

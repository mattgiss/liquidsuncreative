Something for you. Issue#8

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

"Sometimes I forget that life isn’t just calendars, calls, and caffeine. Then I step outside, touch some rocks, nearly tip over a vehicle, and boom—instant reset. We spend so much time trying to control everything, but moments of calculated chaos? That’s where the memories live. Whether it’s a trail, a game, or a spontaneous trip with your family—lean into the wild stuff. "

1 Wallpaper

I don’t usually hit trails after dark—but sometimes the sunset loses and I’m racing headlights to the trailhead. One night on Colorado’s Switzerland Trail (a favorite of mine), I pulled over and caught this view of the city glowing below. Mountains have this sneaky way of humbling you—quietly reminding you that you’re small, but not insignificant. I’ll never get tired of that kind of perspective.

1 Quote From Others

"In every walk with nature, one receives far more than he seeks." — John Muir

1 Question For You

When’s the last time you chose adventure over autopilot?

Something for you. Issue#16

Nice to meet you, My name is Matthew and I have something for you.

The something for you newsletter is an email that focuses on 4 things to help you thrive. Each message includes: 1 insight from me, 1 wallpaper, 1 quote, and 1 question for you to consider.

1 Insight From Me

Book: The War of Art by Steven Pressfield
The War of Art is less a book and more a mirror. Pressfield doesn’t sugarcoat it: your biggest enemy isn’t lack of time, talent, or tools. It’s Resistance—that invisible force that shows up the moment you sit down to build, write, launch, or lead.

Resistance is clever. It disguises itself as procrastination, overplanning, even perfectionism. But its only goal? To keep you exactly where you are.

Pressfield makes it clear: the cure is professionalism. Show up. Every day. No excuses. Art isn’t born in bursts of inspiration—it’s chiseled out of routine, discipline, and a willingness to do the work even when the muse ghosts you.

This book isn’t about creativity. It’s about commitment. Because if you're waiting to feel ready, you're already losing the war.

1 Quote From Others

"Resistance will tell you anything to keep you from doing your work. It will perjure, fabricate; it will seduce you. Resistance is insidious. It will reason with you like a lawyer or jam a knife in your heart like a thug. Resistance is always lying and always full of shit.”– Steven Pressfield

1 Question For You

What important work have you been putting off... and what if showing up was enough to beat it?

About

A Colorado studio with a Part 107 in the air and 15 years behind the lens.

Liquid Sun Creative is led by Matthew Gissentanna Sr., an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot, geospatial analyst, and filmmaker based in Brighton, Colorado. We deliver aerial imaging and visual content for clients across the U.S. — combining technical expertise with artistic vision.

Whether the brief is construction progress documentation, real estate visuals, asset inspections, or branded marketing content, we run full-service aerial and ground-based production. Fully insured for commercial operations, with a network that lets us travel to where the story is.
